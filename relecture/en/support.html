A question about the prototype, a file that will not open, a detail to improve? Write directly to the developer.

Help us understand

Include the Relecture version, your iPhone model, iOS version, what you were trying to do and the message shown. A screenshot with your texts and personal information hidden may help. Do not send passwords, keys or confidential text.

Protect your work

Before uninstalling the app or working with its files, export your work from Relecture if you want to keep it. Exports are in plain text: store them in a trusted location. If a draft can no longer be read, contact us before deleting files.

Ask to test

The prototype is not yet published. To join the tests, simply describe how you review texts today and which iPhone you use. No private document is needed for this first request.
