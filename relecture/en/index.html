How it works

Read a revision
between the lines.

A client message, a description, a revised draft. Relecture brings both versions together to help you find what changed.

01 / Choose

The original and its revision.

Paste both texts or import a UTF-8 TXT or Markdown file. You stay in control of the content.

02 / Examine

The changes, in context.

Move through additions and removals. Choose the amounts, dates, links and exact phrases you want to preserve.

03 / Resume

A draft you can return to.

Your draft stays on the iPhone. Return to your texts, copy the revision or export your work.

Questions

The details,
plainly.

Something else on your mind?
Write to the developer

Does Relecture correct my text?

The prototype compares the original and revision you provide. It does not generate a new version or certify factual accuracy. Its markers help you review; you choose what to change.

Where do my texts stay?

Processing and the draft stay on your device. No iCloud sync or connection to an AI model is configured. Exported files contain the texts in plain text: choose a trusted location. Read the privacy policy.

Is this an AI text detector?

No. Relecture inspects some Unicode characters and encodings; that cannot establish who wrote a text. No Claude or OpenAI statistical watermark detector is available in the app, and removal of those watermarks is not promised.

Which files can I use?

Current imports accept UTF-8 TXT and Markdown. JSON exports let you recover your work. Comparison and analysis accept up to 40,000 UTF-16 units per text; exceeding the limit is reported, never silently truncated. PDF and OCR are not part of the current prototype.

Can I download the app now?

Relecture is a private iPhone prototype for iOS 18 and later. It is not yet published on the App Store. You can ask to join the tests; arrangements and availability will be explained to you.
