How it works

Read a revision
between the lines.

A client message, a description, a revised draft. Orlet brings both versions together to help you find what changed.

01 / Choose

The original and its revision.

Paste both texts or import a file. From Notes and compatible apps, iOS sharing prepares a copy that you confirm in Orlet. The source document stays unchanged.

02 / Examine

The changes, in context.

Move through additions and removals. Check occurrence differences for numbers, dates and links; add currencies, names and exact phrases to your elements to preserve.

03 / Resume

Work you can return to.

Your draft stays on the iPhone. Copy the revision or export both texts and their elements to preserve as JSON. With Pro, find your documents, versions and profiles in the local library.

Does Orlet correct my text?

Orlet compares the original and revision you provide. It does not generate a new version or certify factual accuracy. Its markers help you review; you choose what to change.

Where do my texts stay?

Processing, the draft, library, versions and profiles stay on your device. Orlet does not sync them through iCloud or send your texts to an AI model. Exported files contain the texts in plain text: choose a trusted location. Read the privacy policy.

Is this an AI text detector?

No. Orlet inspects some Unicode characters and encodings; that cannot establish who wrote a text. No Claude or OpenAI statistical watermark detector is available in the app, and removal of those watermarks is not promised.

Which files can I use?

In the app, import UTF-8 TXT or Markdown. iOS sharing also supports RTF and PDFs containing text. JSON exports let you recover your work. Comparison and analysis accept up to 40,000 UTF-16 units per text; exceeding the limit is reported, never silently truncated. Scanned PDFs, Word and Pages are not converted directly; no OCR is included.

What remains available after Pro?

Comparison and inspection remain free. Existing documents stay readable, openable, recoverable as copies and exportable; profiles stay readable. Creating documents or versions and creating or reusing profiles requires Pro. A subscription does not sync your documents between devices.

Can I download the app now?

App Store publication of Orlet is being prepared, for iPhone on iOS 18 and later. No download link is offered here yet. Contact the developer with availability questions.
