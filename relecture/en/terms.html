The prototype

Relecture is an iPhone prototype developed by Mehdi Oudghiri. It is not yet published on the App Store. Participation in tests depends on the arrangements explained to you; no subscription can be purchased through this site.

Use and limits

The app compares the texts provided and performs literal checks. It does not certify factual accuracy, authorship or the origin of a text. Unicode inspection is not a Claude or OpenAI statistical watermark detector. Detecting and removing those watermarks is not part of the current service.

You remain responsible for the content you import, its use and reviewing it before sending. Only import content you are authorized to use. Do not share another person’s private data without permission.

Your texts

You keep your rights to your texts. Current processing takes place on the device. Source files are not modified and exports are in plain text. See the privacy policy for retention and contact details.

Local library limits

The pilot accepts up to 100 documents, 20 versions per document and 20 personal profiles. The library file is limited to 64 MB. Reaching a limit does not automatically delete or replace existing work. The app’s size limits and messages apply to imports and requested operations.
