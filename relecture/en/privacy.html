Privacy

Your texts stay on your device. This page describes the prototype and its test-purchase pilot.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who develops Relecture?

Relecture is developed by Mehdi Oudghiri, an independent iOS developer. For data-related questions: oudghiri_mehdi@icloud.com.

Texts, drafts and documents

You choose the texts you paste or import. Comparison, literal checks and Unicode inspection run on your device. Texts, elements to preserve and drafts are stored locally. The library, saved versions and personal profiles are also stored locally.

Texts are not sent to an analysis server, OpenAI, Anthropic or RevenueCat. No provider detector is connected to the prototype. No iCloud document sync is configured.

Imports and exports

You choose a file through the Files picker to import it. Source files are not modified. Exports contain your texts in plain text; choose a trusted location. If you choose iCloud Drive or another storage provider, its service and rules apply to the file you put there.

An unreadable draft may be kept locally before recovery so that your work is not silently deleted.

Apple and RevenueCat test-purchase pilot

Public purchases are not enabled. Some private builds may enable Apple Sandbox test purchases and their reporting to RevenueCat. A Sandbox transaction is a test, not real revenue. Apple handles confirmation and transactions; Pro access is determined by verified Apple transactions.

In these builds, RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous technical identifier and purchase data: product and transaction identifiers, subscription history and status, the test environment, and price and currency when available. The SDK also transmits technical information needed for the service, such as app, operating system and SDK versions. Connections to the service may reveal the IP address. This data is used to verify and analyze test purchases.

No texts, documents, profiles or reports are sent to RevenueCat. No name or email is added as a customer attribute. Automatic device identifier collection and SDK diagnostics are disabled in the prepared integration. The configuration includes no advertising tracking.

Restoring purchases may synchronize the app’s Apple purchase history with RevenueCat again. Deleting a local document or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete the technical purchase record held by Apple or RevenueCat and does not cancel a subscription. Deleting that record from within the app is not implemented; contact the developer for a request. See RevenueCat’s privacy information.

Before public activation, this policy and the Apple disclosure must match the selected build. Required information and permissions will be provided according to the configuration actually enabled.

Measurement and advertising

The prototype adds no advertising tracking or external measurement of texts or review actions. The pilot purchase reporting described above is separate. This site uses no analytics cookies, advertising pixels, remote fonts or analytics scripts.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information needed for hosting and security, such as visitors’ IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

When you contact us

The contact button opens your email app; it sends nothing automatically. If you send a message, we receive the address and information you choose to include, only to answer and handle your request. Avoid sending private texts, passwords or keys.

Retention and control

Local data remains in the app while you keep it. Export a copy before uninstalling if you want to keep your work. Files you have exported or sent must be managed in their respective locations.

To request access, correction or deletion of information sent to support, email oudghiri_mehdi@icloud.com. We will respond under applicable rules based on the information we actually hold.

Changes to the app

An added service, synchronization or new collection will be described before activation. This page does not present a service in preparation as already active.
