The developer and license

Louevo is developed by Mehdi Oudghiri. Support: contact@moodapps.fr.

For the version distributed through the App Store, the license is Apple’s standard license agreement, unless a specific agreement is presented later. This page explains how Louevo works and the Pro offer; it does not replace Apple’s terms applicable to purchases or rights provided by law.

The service

Louevo maintains an equipment register, prepares rentals and tracks returns, including partial returns, as well as incidents and unavailability. Quantities, dates, names, notes, rates and photos come from your entries. Check this information and the actual condition of equipment before making a commitment to a client.

The rates you enter are used to estimate a rental. Louevo does not collect payments from your clients or deposits, and does not issue invoices. A PDF summarizes your data; it is not automatically a signed rental contract or certified evidence.

Your register and iCloud

Your register is stored locally, available offline and automatically synced to your private iCloud database when sync is enabled and the service is available. Sync is enabled by default. Stock, rentals, returns and their history, photos, kits and drafts are reconciled across your devices using the same iCloud account. The developer does not access the contents of your private database in production.

You can turn off “Sync with iCloud” in Settings → iCloud to stop future transfers on this device without deleting local or remote copies. A transfer already in progress may finish. This preference is local and does not disable syncing on your other devices.

When sync is enabled, the app attempts a transfer after your changes and when it opens. While it is used in the foreground, it also checks remote changes approximately every 45 seconds. Open Louevo on the other device to access the latest changes. Sync requires a connection, an available iCloud account and sufficient storage space; immediate or background receipt is not guaranteed. The full copy is limited to 10 MB, including photos and history. Local changes are retained if the transfer fails.

Independent changes are reconciled. If versions are incompatible or there is a stock conflict, sync is paused and both copies are retained for review. An incompatible version does not overwrite your register. Changing the iCloud account also pauses transfers for the register linked to the previous account.

iCloud keeps a copy of the current register, without being a backup of all its earlier versions. A synced deletion may appear on other devices. You can check iCloud status in Settings → iCloud. Manual export and restore tools are reserved for the recovery flow when a problem is detected; no manual copy is required as a normal step in syncing.

The pilot and existing data

Installations already recognized as part of the pilot keep the included tools on that installation. This access is recorded locally, is separate from an Apple subscription and is not synced across devices. Deleting and reinstalling the app may cause this local recognition to be lost; syncing or recovering the register and restoring purchases do not automatically restore it. If you are unsure, contact support before uninstalling the app or subscribing.

New installations have access to the free core and can subscribe to Pro for additional tools. Equipment, reservations, returns, drafts, the journal, iCloud sync, recovery tools, viewing data and processing rentals already in progress remain available for free.

Activating Pro or the end of a subscription does not delete saved data. Neither purchases nor Pro or pilot entitlements are transferred into the iCloud register. Subscription entitlements are checked separately by Apple and RevenueCat. Before changing devices or deleting the app, check the last confirmed transfer and that your register is present on the other device. Deleting the app does not automatically delete the iCloud copy.

The Louevo Pro offer

Pro covers kit creation and duplication to prepare regular rentals, PDF generation and adding new photos. Both plans provide the same Pro features.

  • Monthly: France reference price of €9.99 for one month.
  • Annual: France reference price of €79.99 for one year, paid in one payment.

These amounts correspond to the France reference catalog. Before any purchase, Louevo shows the localized price provided by Apple and the selected period. The Apple sheet confirms the amount charged. A price on this page is not used as a fallback price in the app. No free trial is planned for these offers.

Payment and renewal

The purchase is confirmed with Apple through your Apple account. The monthly plan renews for one month and the annual plan for one year until renewal is stopped in your Apple account. The billing period, amount and terms are presented before confirmation.

To avoid a new period, turn off renewal before its deadline in Apple subscription management. The exact billing time and renewal conditions are those indicated by Apple. Stopping renewal does not normally end the period already paid for immediately. Refund requests are handled through Apple’s tools under their conditions; Louevo does not guarantee acceptance.

Restores, pending states and access

“Restore purchases” checks the entitlements of the Apple account that made the purchase without starting a new purchase. “Check my purchase” can repeat verification of a confirmed purchase without asking you to pay again. An Apple request awaiting approval does not grant Pro before confirmation.

A network issue does not delete the register and does not, by itself, remove the last recognized entitlement. On expiration or after a refund is actually recognized, subscription status may become inactive. Data and core tools remain accessible; features already included in the pilot are retained.

Apple Sandbox tests

Development and TestFlight versions use Apple Sandbox. The App Store version uses real purchases. Test subscriptions may have accelerated durations. For a Sandbox test, only confirm an Apple sheet that explicitly identifies the purchase as a test with no charge. A Sandbox test is neither a real payment nor evidence of commercial revenue.

Support and privacy

If a problem occurs, contact support with the app version and the message shown. The privacy policy explains local information, private iCloud sync, recovery copies and limited subscription processing by Apple and RevenueCat.
